No founder, developer, or administrator should have unilateral access to withdraw or redirect protected funds. Resources are intended for verified humanitarian needs, including food, clean water, and essential supplies.



# AMANAT — A Trust That Cannot Be Personally Withdrawn

A transparent, open-source framework for protecting humanitarian funds and supporting people living in poverty.

## 1. Vision
AMANAT is a proposed blockchain-based humanitarian protection system designed to ensure that resources dedicated to people in need cannot be treated as the personal property of a founder, developer, administrator, or individual contributor.

**Funds committed to helping people in poverty must be protected by rules, not by personal promises.**

## 2. Core Principles
- Protection from the beginning: designate humanitarian funds under explicit, verifiable rules.
- No personal withdrawal rights: no unilateral founder or administrator withdrawals to personal wallets.
- Transparent accounting with beneficiary privacy.
- Humanitarian priorities: food, clean water, essential supplies, and urgent needs.
- Independent oversight and security audits.
- Open-source accountability.

## 3. Intended Operation
1. Humanitarian funds enter a dedicated smart-contract-controlled system.
2. Protected funds are separated from ordinary project funds.
3. Unauthorized personal withdrawals are prohibited by contract rules.
4. Permitted payments go through a defined process, such as verified aid recipients or food suppliers.
5. Public records and independent audits support accountability.

## 4. Security by Design
Exclude hidden withdrawal privileges, unrestricted administrative overrides, and unilateral changes that undermine protections. Carefully review upgrade and emergency mechanisms. Permanently locked funds cannot also be used for ongoing aid, so allocations must be designed accordingly.

## 5. Project Status and Limitations
AMANAT is a proposed design, not an already deployed or audited financial system. Security, legal compliance, beneficiary verification, and delivery of aid must be tested and independently reviewed before real funds are accepted. Blockchain transparency alone does not guarantee that aid reaches the right people.

## 6. Long-Term Goal
Build a transparent framework to direct resources toward people who need them most, without giving any individual—including the founder—unrestricted personal control over protected humanitarian funds.

**Protect the purpose. Preserve human dignity. Make every permitted use accountable.**
